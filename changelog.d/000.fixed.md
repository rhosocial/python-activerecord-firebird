Fixed a segmentation fault caused by closing firebird-driver connections from `__del__` during garbage collection: backends and connections are now strongly referenced until explicitly disconnected.

Corrected dialect capabilities: Firebird supports only UNION (no INTERSECT/EXCEPT), has no DSQL EXPLAIN statement, and stores TIMESTAMP with 1/10000 s precision; tests now adapt accordingly.

Mapped Firebird NOT NULL / validation errors (`SQLSTATE 23000`, `sqlcode -625`) to `IntegrityError`.

Added a pool `validation_query` and scenario filtering for the `--scenarios` option.
